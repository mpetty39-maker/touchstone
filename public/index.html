How to play

Tap a gem to preview where the glowing shape lands, then tap the same gem again to place it. The shape flips every gem it covers: lit goes dark, dark lights up. The gold-ringed square marks the gem you tap.

Shapes go in order, so you can't use one twice in a row. Light every gem to clear the level. There are five levels and the board grows. First to clear all five wins. If time runs out, whoever got farthest wins.
